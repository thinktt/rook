## Exact geometry and retained scalar work

For each color derive occupied=plane0|plane1|plane2, friendly=occupied&ownColor and canonical piece masks from the three tag planes. Derive bishop opaque, rook opaque and rook-forward opaque only when that actual piece group is nonempty. Bishop transparency is all enemy occupants plus own N/R; rook transparency is enemy nonpawns plus own R, with own N/B/Q additionally transparent on the single forward vertical direction. Queen transparency is zero. Do not substitute coherent Influence transparency (own Q/B/R, opposing king, pawn-extra and weakening): it has different semantics.

For the original direction's ray R, let P be prefix through the nearest occupied bit inclusive; X prefix through nearest nontransparent occupied bit inclusive. No blocker yields whole R. Direct D=P&!friendly; additional A=(X&!P)&!friendly. Directions with positive df+8dr take the lowest set bit; negative take highest. Prefix arithmetic must guard zero and use wrapping-safe first-bit masks. Enumerate each ray's D then A near-to-far; preserve source-list, piece-kind, color and direction chronology. Transparent friendly blockers are omitted but allow A continuation; the first enemy is D even when transparent; later enemy/empty A endpoints continue until an opaque occupant, whose friendly/enemy admission is unchanged.

At every D target fetch the real mailbox victim and call existing direct_category/capture_priority; preserve categories C, category-weighted T, immediate weak/strong mark (including category0), and rook vertical T multiplier2. At every A target increment exactly one query BEFORE existing unadjusted relation/capture_priority, add T, no C and no mark. Retain all supplied Influence bytes, per-piece score/source penalty, per-color rounding, material/phase/weights, potential king-pressure and retained/evaluator charge/observation order. No destination/source union, category planes, packed Influence decoding, bulk popcount charge or delayed mark flushing. Keep scalar knight geometry initially.

A single ray mask can contain blockers of either sign; clearing the wrong prefix bit or including a second friendly opaque endpoint is a real correctness risk. Immutable source rays depend only on source<64. Public activity on malformed/stale positions must retain its original scalar table/panic paths; never validate/reject/normalize new public inputs or predecode kinds before old lazy accesses. True geometry admission belongs only to the already-guarded owned chain, with exact fallback otherwise.

## Costs, alternatives and decision evidence

Required setup includes deriving color/type/opaque masks, retaining borrowed planes and masks, ray lookup, first occupied and first opaque selection, prefix operations and near-to-far bit scans, plus unchanged victim loads/classifier/mark work. Worst-case two nearest-blocker operations are paid even on short/empty rays; query count is not a CPU-cost model. Without sliders, derive no slider masks. Avoid eager all-color/all-kind setup and copied32-byte planes. A borrowed pointer is not guaranteed to eliminate emitted copies or frame growth; call ABI and inlining must be inspected on the complete supply+consumer.

Keeping mailbox traversal has no new permission plumbing and sequential ordered target loads; it is the control and may win sparse/endgame lists. A local mailbox-only scan/transpose would preserve public stale-plane authority but pays64-square construction/alias decoding every invocation, repeating part of35's setup; do not select it implicitly as fallback.
