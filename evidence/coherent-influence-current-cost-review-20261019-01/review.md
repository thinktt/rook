Required semantics for any later feasibility work:

- Keep each source's disjoint normal/weak masks and cross-source multiplicity. OR-union across sources loses attackers. Add16/4/2 for B/R/Q normal targets and1 for weak targets, with exact modulo256 byte arithmetic. Carries across packed tiers are intentional; no digitwise saturation or capped physical counts may replace them.
- Preserve existing transparency, opposing-king passage, included stopping square, forward own-pawn extra and first-own-queen weakening. These costs stay in the required mask owner.
- Preserve the existing scalar P/N/K contributions. If they remain in bytes, a slider accumulator must combine its final bytes with them by wrapping addition or pay an input transpose. No leaper contribution may disappear. Folding leapers into a new accumulator would be additional scope, not a free extension.
- No-slider/sparse positions must pay all real setup/finalization costs. Eight live scalar counters compete with the geometry kernel's GPRs, versus the current four XMM sums; spills and the full final transpose can erase any benefit. Pure source guards do not guarantee physical zero setup, as42 demonstrated.
- Keep the existing coherent permission and public scalar/custom-map bypass. No persistent fields, caching, new authority or changed map-build/charge events are needed merely to ask this output question.

The necessary discriminator is an exact arithmetic/transpose feasibility and concrete emitted-cost comparison that includes accumulation, all live state and final conversion on zero/low/dense slider inventory. Current40 complete emitter lowering, spill behavior and per-color-versus-per-source intrinsic assignment are not established by these source-line profiles.
