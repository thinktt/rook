## Exact per-ray formulation

For each existing direction, R is the same geometric ray mask, O occupancy, F own occupancy, T the transparent occupied-piece mask. Let P=prefix(R,O), X=prefix(R,O&!T), where prefix includes the nearest blocker, or the entire ray if there is none. Then D=P&!F and A=(X&!P)&!F. Because opaque blockers are a subset of occupants, P is contained in X. D is direct and A strictly beyond its first occupant. Queen has T=0 and therefore A=0.

Bishop T=all opposing occupied squares OR own knights/rooks. Rook T=opposing nonpawns OR own rooks; only pawn-forward vertical direction additionally admits own knights/bishops/queens. Opposing kings remain admitted/transparent where specified. First opposing transparent occupant is DIRECT: adjusted relation/shortcut/capture priority and a mark occur before switching to additional. Every later included opponent is ADDITIONAL: unadjusted relation plus capture priority and exactly one R, including a final opaque opposing pawn on a rook ray. A transparent own occupant is excluded but allows continuation; opaque own occupant ends the ray excluded. Multiple transparent occupants, empty gaps and edges must all follow these rules. Do not substitute Influence ray weakening or legal/pin/check safety.

Use the existing attacks.rs MASKS.rays and increasing directions, not another4096B ray table. Nearest blocker is low bit for positive df+8dr, high bit for negative. Guard zero before first-bit extraction. Positive prefix uses R&(b|(b-1)); negative uses R&!(b-1); no blocker returns R.
