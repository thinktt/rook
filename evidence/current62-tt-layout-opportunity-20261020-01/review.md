## Footprint, alternatives, next stop

For exactlyGgroups, slab payload increases48G→64G: +16G,33⅓%. Fixed semantic group count cannot be lowered to repay that cost. More allocation/residency/page/TLB footprint and possible cache-capacity/conflict costs apply even if fewer lines are touched per selected group. from_tts161–166 uses fallible reserve then initialization; with_groups156–159 and wrap clear172–179 also operate on the physical slab. Preserve lazy engine allocation/replacement atengine134–139 and generation-only usual clearing. Actual padding stores/zeroing widths require compiler evidence; do not assume every padding byte is written or ignored. Allocation failure may occur at budgets previously allocatable; no released physical-byte ceiling was established. parameters101–131 admits native tts>=96 within its integer representation; released joined diagnostics have their own narrower capacities. A physical layout change must disclose the higher resource requirement and preserve errors, not silently clamp G.

A compact per-group SoA/key-first layout might keep validity/identity filters together without33% expansion, but introduces offset/packing or multiple bases and separates match payload/depth mutations; it is a different source/codegen question, not evidence that64B padding is free. Wider record loads can also trade instruction count against split accesses; retain safe bytes/alignment and all short-circuit/metadata order. Do not choose a layout matrix now. Current Group48 compile-time/test assertions are implementation checks; a selected physical alternative needs explicit reviewed adaptation, not a false claim of literal unchanged layout tests.
