**Collision induction.** Regard each state only through its prescribed pair (group,secondary). Starting empty, store reads/writes only that group. Within a group, at most one valid retained record has a given secondary: a matching store replaces its existing retained priority or writes recent; an unmatched store inserts into an empty or displaced priority. Recent may duplicate it by design. Consequently probe's first retained match is the unique retained result. This induction is independent of whether equal numeric pairs originate from the same board, distinct boards, rights/EP states, histories, or a modulo-primary collision. A same-group/different-secondary record cannot contribute score or metadata. Equal-secondary/different-group records cannot interact. Equal pairs intentionally share the result identity; adding a board witness would alter the normative behavior. A group collision alone is not an identity match; secondary equality alone outside that group is not a match. No empirical collision-frequency or global hash uniqueness assertion is needed.

All collisions then use the same abstract retention rules already reviewed for opaque diagnostic labels. A strictly shallower matching store leaves retained untouched and replaces recent. Equal/deeper replacement leaves an older recent duplicate intact. New identities use first empty before any signed depth, otherwise the first least-depth retained priority; equality displaces. Probe consults recent before retained and stops on a usable recent result. A no-score recent can contribute a hint/quiet/marked value but the later retained record owns the final bound; mate depth raising modifies the actual recent or retained record after observing metadata. Generation validity makes old epochs invisible, including identity0. Lifecycle timing and numerical capacities are already separate accepted proofs, not re-established by finite fixtures here.
