# Placement masks plus wrapping SIMD Influence — source-only design

**A complete producer can remove scalar target traversal, but dense SIMD emission is not yet a selected trial. The defensible opportunity is the combination of ray-mask geometry and byte-lane accumulation, not a substitution of vector adds alone.** Current25 has meaningful constructor attribution on35 and much less target work on08. A whole-profile emitter paid per pawn/king can readily consume the latter budget. Recommend retaining this as a bounded kernel-design option; if selected, require actual construction-inclusive lowering and complete byte parity before a fixed pilot. No new census, cache, field, source edit or prototype is proposed here.

## Exact formulation

Current `src/influence.rs:35–92` selects emitter side/type and multiplicity from chronological `piece_squares`, while `:159–202` reads the actual mailbox for ray blockers. Keep those authorities. For one slider direction let R be its geometric ray. Transparent occupants are enemy kings, friendly queens, and friendly bishops on diagonals or rooks on orthogonals; all other occupied squares are opaque. Let P include R through its nearest opaque occupant, or all R when none. If that occupant is a friendly pawn on a pawn-forward diagonal, extend P by exactly the next on-ray square, irrespective of its occupant; do not inspect that extra square to continue again. This is V, the complete visited set.

Let q be the nearest friendly queen **inside V**. Let N be V through q inclusive, or V if no q; W=V−N. Add original contribution c to N, weak1 to W. The first queen itself retains the old c; a second queen/matching slider never restores c. An opposing king remains transparent; the own king is opaque and included. A queen on the single pawn-extra square receives the current contribution but cannot extend V. All eight directions from one source are disjoint, so their N/W masks may be unioned separately for that source. Never union different emitting sources: duplicated lists and overlap must still add repeatedly. Pawns/knights/kings use their complete fixed geometric masks without occupancy filtering.

`attacks.rs:14–72` already owns immutable rays/king/knight/pawn-source masks and step polarity; its Boolean `attacked` result is **not** this geometry. A future pure mask accessor could reuse those immutable masks and tag planes without new stored fields. Friendly-queen weakening and pawn extension still require additional nearest-blocker/queen work; replacing the target loop does not eliminate all direction processing. `geometry.rs` remains authoritative for direction/color orientation. No legal-attack first-blocker helper can substitute unchanged.

## Complete SIMD emission and costs

A 2 KiB immutable expansion table maps each8-bit target fragment to eight byte lanes of0/1. Pair adjacent fragments into four16-byte vectors for one64-square color profile. All c values are powers of two (1,2,4,16,64); constant 16-bit lane shifts of these0/1 bytes are exact through shift6 (no byte overflows), or use0xff table bytes AND a contribution splat. Combine disjoint N/W increments, then `_mm_add_epi8` into four color accumulators. Byte wrapping exactly implements the released mixed-radix normalization modulo256, including discarded pawn carry. Saturating adds, integer64-bit adds with inter-byte carry, OR across sources, or saturation before accumulation would be wrong. Adjusted-relation saturation remains in its unchanged consumer.

One emitter need update only its own **64B color**, not necessarily both128B profiles. Four live vector accumulators per side could avoid per-piece output load/stores and materialize128B once at completion. That is an unproved lowering hypothesis: expansion loads, packing, weight operations, root/caller register pressure and spills remain real. A straightforward one-mask emitter still needs eight table loads plus packing/four vector additions for64 lanes; two masks can double expansion work even if increments merge before those additions. Pawns have at most two scalar destinations, knights/kings at most eight. SIMD has no baseline arbitrary byte scatter or SSSE3 shuffle here. Do not assume table/code locality, four accumulator residency, zero copies, or profitability from arithmetic equivalence. Keeping the old scalar target walk merely to assemble masks would miss most of the geometric opportunity.

## Domain and lifetime obstacles

Private `AttackBits` is placement-coherent on the intended native transition path, but root validation is a conditional premise, not a property of every public call. `Position` exposes board/lists/counts (`position.rs:23–25`); `Influence::build` currently accepts list/mailbox combinations directly. Source lists may name ghost/duplicate emitters, high-bit tags can have valid color/kind aliases, and direct mailbox edits can leave private planes stale. Reconstructing emitters from plane occupancy changes current behavior. Eager invalid-tag decoding can also introduce a panic for an unvisited tag. A complete public replacement needs faithful mailbox-derived view plus fallback, or a separately proved trusted internal path with the public builder unchanged; a fresh64-square guard/reconstruction has a cost. No such trust seam is selected. `Position::validate` explicitly checks lists/mailbox/planes (`:425–457`); its existence does not establish that every caller used it.

`Evaluator::ensure_influence` (`evaluator.rs:296–305`) builds only on None. Supplied Some maps must remain untouched, including synthetic maps; this proposal cannot rebuild them from placement or change lazy fill/DIAGNOSTICS event order. Influence construction adds no relation R; keep all consumer charge/query decisions, pawn/cache/retained/observer chronology and final128B API unchanged. Wrapping sums commute, so coherent chronology may change without numerical change; public malformed-input short-circuit/failure behavior still needs preservation.
