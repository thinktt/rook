Fresh43 computes complete maps. Per color, zero sliders retains scalar P/N/K; otherwise it initializes eight u64 counter planes, adds all P/N/K and slider masks modulo256, then transposes once into64 bytes. This is the actual competitor, not old29/30. See W43 `coherent.rs:15–74,132–201`.50 instead copies the authenticated parent into final output, removes the full old mover/victim, adds the full new mover including promotions, discovers every affected unchanged source/direction and computes its old/new shared masks, cancels equal normal/weak weights, then scans remaining targets for byte add/sub (`delta.rs:9–43,48–105`). Full source geometry, inverse-ray discovery, old/new placement classification and private map/child/coherence proof remain paid. Q stays fresh. Cancellation is already present; the next representation cannot count it again as new savings.

The unstarted alternative accumulates **only signed changes** into zeroed local bitplanes, transposes the delta, then wrapping-adds the borrowed native parent's bytes. There is no inverse transpose of the parent and no retained parent-plane state. Modulo256 makes signed change exact: at bit b, XOR updates the plane; carry propagates through old1 bits for addition, borrow through old0 bits for subtraction. Discard the outgoing bit8. For one nonempty weight2^s mask, an ordinary fixed-width implementation touches8−s planes and has7−s carry/borrow propagations: Weak1=8/7, Queen2=7/6, Rook4=6/5, Minor16=4/3, Pawn64=2/1. These are source-algebra units, not instruction estimates. A signed counterpart to43's merged normal/weak emitter needs its own proof; equal-weight cancellation must still retain normal↔weak transitions and multiplicity across sources.

Relative to50, such an emitter can replace target-popcount scans/byte read-modify-writes with mask-wide signed carry/borrow operations. It removes neither full mover/victim/new-mover mask construction nor selected-ray old/new math/discovery. The simplest two-color route adds16 zeroed counter words, two forward transposes and128 wrapping parent-byte additions (eight packed SSE2 additions if implemented that way). Each existing forward transpose has12 butterfly swaps:72 scalar shift/XOR/AND operations in the literal source, then8 64-bit-to-XMM transfers,12 unpacks and4 stores, excluding loads and compiler folding. Reusing one64-byte counter bank per color is possible in principle; it is not an emitted frame or spill proof. Eight live GPR planes also compete with two mask views and bit-scan setup.

Output ownership matters. Keeping50's already-parent-initialized API adds a delta output/temporary plus dense wrapping addition on top of its parent copy. An alternative producer could transpose into the final payload and then add the immutable parent, replacing the copy with a dense add pass; fusing addition into transpose stores could change further reads/stores. Neither erasure is established by algebra or current code. Such a contract/store-owner change would need separate review, not a copy-saving assumption. No new architecture is proposed here.
