2. **The capability needs three independent premises.**

It must establish (a) this immutable map is the native result for this exact parent placement, (b) parent and child mailbox, source inventories and maintained planes are coherent for the mask kernel, and (c) this actual child is the specified two-square transition. Your existing geometry permission can establish(b) along its guarded native chain; the new map association establishes(a). Neither replaces the other.

In particular, “minted at a real native build” is too broad if it includes a fresh public scalar build without geometry permission. [ensure_influence_for_search](/home/legolas/rook2/src/evaluator.rs:316) can build scalar with permission false; that authenticates the scalar result but does not validate its geometry. The existing [stale-mailbox test](/home/legolas/rook2/src/engine/geometry_tests.rs:77) deliberately exercises precisely that distinction. A genuine child and genuine scalar parent map do not make stale planes safe for slider_masks. Keep such chains on the existing fallback unless coherence is separately established. This needs no repeated validation of already guarded descendants.

Bind the capability to live immutable position/map borrows, not an independently reusable boolean, ply slot or public Some. Missing incoming provenance need not poison later descendants: a fresh map constructed under established geometry can seed a new trusted chain. Conversely, a valid geometry flag cannot authenticate a supplied map.

3. **The affected-set proof survives joined setups; cancellation is the interesting refinement.**

For an unchanged slider origin/direction, its contribution reads only that ray. The friendly-pawn extra square remains on it. If neither changed square lies on that ray, its contribution is unchanged. Remove/add mover and victim emissions separately, exclude their origins from unchanged-source replay, and deduplicate directions. All collinear origins of both colors remain necessary through transparent sliders, queens and opposing kings; king moves and promotions do not invalidate this reasoning. Wrapping removal remains exact even when the parent byte overflowed. EP/castling exclusions avoid additional changed squares.

Full-source slider_masks replay preserves one numerical owner but evaluates unaffected directions. More importantly, subtracting every old target and adding every new target repeats common work even on an affected direction. For each source and contribution weight, equal old/new target membership can cancel before scattering. A normal-to-weak change must still remove the old weight and add the new one; do not cancel merely because both masks contain that target. Never union contributions across distinct sources. Cancellation costs mask operations too, so it is a distinct hypothesis, not a promised improvement.

4. **An omitted representation alternative avoids inverse transposition.**

One can accumulate only the signed change into zeroed temporary bitplanes modulo256, transpose that delta once, then wrapping-add the borrowed parent's bytes. This does not inverse-transpose the parent or retain planes across recursion. It could use43's existing target masks and forward transpose, with a separately justified signed accumulation operation. It still pays carry/borrow work, a transpose, a dense byte addition and local storage; sparse byte scatter may be cheaper. I suggest recording this alternative, not starting another variant. It shows that “byte scatter versus inverse transpose versus persistent planes” does not exhaust the space.

5. **Measure the proposed demand boundary, including paths that never use the payload.**

The census should separately identify ordinary, IIO, main-to-Q and Q-to-Q requests under the exact intended admission rule. Checked status alone is not disqualifying; missing actual parent materialization is. Q can acquire its current map late through destination support, so optional parent association must reflect availability at the actual child call, without forcing earlier siblings or checked/fast-only entries to create one. Keep affected-set discovery and child materialization at existing demand. A scout retry may recompute the same map while preserving its independently refreshed Retained.

Useful census output pairs eligible demand counts with fresh43 work features: slider/non-slider colors, unchanged source/direction count, old/new masks evaluated, target updates before and after same-weight cancellation, and copy/dispatch exposure. Include the incidence of optional transport on entries that return before any map demand. Source counts are not instruction weights;43's bit-sliced accumulation and scalar zero-slider path make that particularly important. No Node growth does not imply no recursive-frame, argument, spill or lifetime cost.

**What would change my assessment:** a census showing a specific new reduction against43 beyond the already-known coverage, followed by evidence that the complete derived-map path plus caller/transport costs beats the actual fresh path in relevant workloads. Independent scalar full-byte differential checking must remain the numerical reference when kernels share masks. Native whole-search own-PGO evidence remains decisive under your existing screen policy; even another large simulated-Ir reduction would not resolve the previous mismatch. Without a new mechanism and plausible complete-cost margin, I would park delta again and compare other owners on concrete removable work rather than their SELF percentages.
