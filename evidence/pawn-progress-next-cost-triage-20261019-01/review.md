## One concrete arithmetic question

Current raw stores measure/preliminary in i32 and uses `/64` then `/8` at78–82. Candidates uses signed `/64` at141 and144 and signed `/16` at174. The divisions' admitted numerators are nonnegative. A possible local representation change would express only this proven score subchain with unsigned arithmetic and convert back before existing signed ending/weight composition; it would leave signed measure, relation, distance, strict ordering and each separate rounding step unchanged. This is distinct from fusion and from the parked activity pressure arithmetic: it changes neither scans nor pressure formulas. The bounded reviewed history contains no trial of this pawn-score representation; that is not a universal search of all evidence.

Bounds follow directly from `pawn-candidates.md:10–24,48–81,83–149`. Relative pawn rank r is2..7, leaving n=8−r forward squares. The nearest backward contribution is at most2. Each forward square adds at most3, with at most one additional promotion-square point. Therefore measure <= r−4+2+3n+1 =23−2r <=19; its lower clamp is−2. Coefficient <=120 gives preliminary <= floor(120*23*11/64)=474, raw <=floor(474*11/8)=651. The race multiplier <=83 and offset <=1440 give intermediate <=866. Clamped kingDistance1..8 yields factor52..73; candidate score <=floor(73*866/64)+400=1387. Top-two total <=floor((28*1387+8)/16)=2427. These intentionally loose bounds fit u32/i32 without overflow and hold even for arbitrary supplied Influence bytes on an otherwise admitted placement. Floor/truncation coincide here. Final pawn ending, asymmetric/signed weights, material balancing and div_euclid at322–362 are signed and must remain so.
