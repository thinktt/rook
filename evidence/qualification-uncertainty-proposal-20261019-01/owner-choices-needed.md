# Choices and validation required before any adoption or launch

1. **Scope and confidence.** Approve the fixed lower-population-median estimand, this one frozen artifact pair /66-unit family, at least95% joint unconditional coverage, and whole-collection13→19 followup with one excluded retained warmup at each executed stage. Freeze how later candidates or separate qualification attempts spend confidence; this draft does not grant unlimited retries or reuse selected screening samples. Existing three-repetition reports cannot be upgraded retroactively.
2. **Repeatability and stationarity.** Choose and validate the exact block/order schedule, CPU/ISA/build/seed/accounting/observation profile, thermal/frequency/load checks and technical repeatability criteria before seeing qualification results. Thirteen or nineteen measurements, a quiet SMT sibling, or an empirical range alone does not prove iid. Correlated scheduler/cache/frequency drift within a unit defeats the sign-tail proof. A prespecified quantitative stability check would be diagnostic evidence, not a mathematical proof of independence; no new numerical tolerance is selected here. No value-based deletion, restarts after a bad spread, or adding observed noise to1.10/1.25.
3. **All-owned accounting.** Validate same-hardware candidate/reference whole-lifetime user+system CPU boundaries, startup/pre-exec prefix, shutdown and any engine threads/delegated work. Excluding supervisor/tap CPU must not hide owned engine work. Specify available PMU/privilege scopes separately; CPU-only uncertainty does not prove any required richer profile. Resolve short-case timer quantization and strictly positive denominators. These are the existing measurement-policy.md:39–61,143–180 obligations, not consequences of the probability bound.
4. **Validity and finite resources.** Freeze exact original input/oracle/assertion identities, limits for each setup/search/lifetime and aggregate wall time, output/artifact bytes, memory/process ownership and five-second owned cleanup before launch [measurement-policy.md:203–222]. An invalid run stops its authorized batch and is retained, with no automatic retry/cap increase. Decide any later invalid-attempt restart authority and its confidence implications independently. No collection is reduced to a favorable completed subset.
5. **Practical budget.** The table below is a fixed count budget, not execution permission or a runtime prediction. Obtain an independent capacity estimate and an explicit host reservation plus finite aggregate cap before adoption. The accepted original3-measured+1-warmup schedule has4 lifetimes per artifact/unit; this proposal uses14 initially (3.5 times as many), and at most21 (5.25 times as many). Long game units dominate elapsed feasibility. A global deadline too small for this schedule must cause incomplete evidence, not a smaller post hoc sample count. The maximum must be affordable *before* using initial data to select a followup.

Fixed per-lifetime caps `S_i` for search cases and `G_j` for game units give a serial native wall-cap budget of `28*(sum S_i + sum G_j)` initially and at most `42*(sum S_i + sum G_j)` in total, before controller/checker/cleanup allowances (one common cap per corresponding unit and artifact; otherwise sum the per-artifact caps). Those worst-case safety bounds are not expected elapsed times. Current runtime estimates are intentionally unset rather than inferred from incomplete live measurements or a universal calibration factor.
