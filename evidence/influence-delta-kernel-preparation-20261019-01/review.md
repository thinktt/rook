**Dependency proof.** [Fresh owner](/home/legolas/rook2/src/influence.rs:35) is a sum of placement-only piece contributions in Z/256Z for each color/target. For changed squares F,T, remove each old piece's complete old-board contribution at F/T, add each new piece's complete child-board contribution there, and exclude F/T from unchanged-origin discovery. The moved slider needs **all** its old/new rays, not merely rays crossing F/T; a captured slider needs all old rays removed. Unchanged pawn/knight/king target sets do not depend on occupancy. An unchanged slider-direction can differ only if its board-reading ray meets F or T: transparency, weakening and the pawn extra step all read on that same geometric ray. Union keys must be `(origin,direction)`, not just origin. Directions paired by `d^1` are inverse in geometry.rs14–23. Discover **all geometric** matching origins, ignoring blockers; the shared old/new ray walker decides relevance. Discovery via first legal attacker/ordered attacker identity is unsound because influence transparency differs from attacks.

For each byte, `new=parent−old_changed+new_changed+Σ(new_affected_ray−old_affected_ray) mod256`. Use physical1/2/4/16/64 weights with wrapping add/sub. Do not decode normalized digits and saturate or use adjusted_relation: [the spec](/home/legolas/king-spec/spec/evaluation-relations.md:15) discards the top carry, whereas adjusted exclusion separately saturates the normalized support. Each ray must still add the current weight **before** examining an occupant; a friendly queen weakens only beyond itself and later transparent pieces never restore weight. Fresh and signed-delta writes should share that exact walker, with the child and parent boards immutable for their respective passes.
