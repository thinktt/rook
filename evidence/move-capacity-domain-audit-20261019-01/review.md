The proof admits unnatural material up to sixteen pieces per color, including the required king, without a legal-game reachability assumption. Queen27 bounds every nonking, including a promoting pawn's at most three destinations times four promotions. Castle contributes at most two. The proof uses neither orthodox218 folklore nor the standalone diagnostic230 ceiling. Setup/current admission retains the ≤16 limit (`position.rs:110–119`); movement, promotion replacement, castle and null do not increase a side's piece count (`:210–225,275–369`).
